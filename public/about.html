Design built to be adored, not just noticed.

Imagine. Inspire. Create.

Two very different brains. One shared eye for good design.

Adeel brings a decade of experience, he understands how businesses think, what people respond to, and how to turn a good idea into something that actually works. A natural problem-solver (and unapologetic perfectionist), he brings the clarity and structure behind the studio.

Tehniyat, a Central Saint Martins Fine Art graduate with a background in the fashion industry, brings an approach that is instinctive, visual and a little unexpected, pulling references from art, fashion, culture and everywhere in between.

Together, they balance strategy with spontaneity and creative chaos with a healthy refusal to do things the obvious way.
